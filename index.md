## About Me

Greetings! I am **Liang Wang**, currently a graduate student at [Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn/en/), advised by Prof. [Zhongyu Wei](http://www.fudan-disc.com/people/zywei). I am a member of the [Data Intelligence and Social Computing Lab (Fudan DISC)](http://fudan-disc.com/) and [Fudan NLP](https://nlp.fudan.edu.cn/). Before joining Fudan, I received my bachelor's degree from [Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen](http://en.hitsz.edu.cn/).

My research centers on Large Language Models, with a particular interest in personalization, user modeling, and post-training. You can find my complete publication record on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=K9KoJt8AAAAJ&hl=en).

## Research Interests

- 🤖 **Personalized Large Language Models** — adapting model behavior to individual users while preserving quality and reliability.
- 👥 **User Modeling** — learning useful representations from heterogeneous and long-term user behavior.
- 🧭 **Alignment and Post-training** — improving model capabilities and preferences through data, evaluation, and reinforcement learning.
