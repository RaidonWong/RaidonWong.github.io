## About Me

Hi! I am **Liang Wang**. My research centers on Large Language Models, with a particular interest in personalization, user modeling, and post-training. This website collects my research updates, publications, projects, and notes.

## Research Interests

- 🤖 **Personalized Large Language Models** — adapting model behavior to individual users while preserving quality and reliability.
- 👥 **User Modeling** — learning useful representations from heterogeneous and long-term user behavior.
- 🧭 **Alignment and Post-training** — improving model capabilities and preferences through data, evaluation, and reinforcement learning.
